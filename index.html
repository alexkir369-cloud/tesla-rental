Rental terms

What you need to collect the car

Paperwork takes 15–20 minutes. The agreement is signed online or at our office; the car is handed over with a photo report.

Documents

EU/EEA driving licence held for at least 3 years, plus an ID card or passport.

Age

23 to 70. Model S Plaid and Cybertruck: from 27 with 5 years of licence.

Deposit

€500–2,000 held on a credit or debit card, released 1–3 days after return.

Payment

Card, SEPA transfer or invoice for companies. All prices include 19% VAT.

Good to know

A few simple rules keep every car in perfect shape:

  • Cross-border travel inside the EU is allowed — just tell us the countries in advance.
  • Smoking, vaping and pets are not allowed (cleaning fee €150).
  • Only drivers named in the agreement may drive the car.
  • Track days, drifting and driving lessons are not allowed.
